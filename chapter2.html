Chapter 2: States of Matter

Pharm D • Physical Pharmacy

What is Matter?

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space. Matter exists mainly as solid, liquid and gas.

1. Solid

Main features:

  • Fixed shape
  • Fixed volume
  • Particles are closely packed
  • Particles have limited movement

Example: Tablet, ice, crystal

2. Liquid

Main features:

  • Fixed volume
  • No fixed shape
  • Takes the shape of its container
  • Particles can move more freely than solids

Example: Syrup, water, liquid medicine

3. Gas

Main features:

  • No fixed shape
  • No fixed volume
  • Particles are far apart
  • Highly compressible

Example: Oxygen, carbon dioxide

Important Difference

Solid: Fixed shape + Fixed volume

Liquid: No fixed shape + Fixed volume

Gas: No fixed shape + No fixed volume

Pharmaceutical Importance

  • Drugs can exist in different physical states.
  • Physical state can affect drug formulation.
  • Particle properties can affect dissolution.
  • Understanding matter helps in preparation and storage of medicines.
