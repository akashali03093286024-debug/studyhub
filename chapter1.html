What is Physical Pharmacy?

Physical Pharmacy is the branch of pharmacy that applies the principles of physics and chemistry to pharmaceutical sciences.

Easy Definition: Physical Pharmacy = Physics + Chemistry applied to Pharmacy.

Importance of Physical Pharmacy

  • Understanding drug solubility
  • Understanding dissolution of drugs
  • Study of pH and buffers
  • Understanding different states of matter
  • Improving drug stability
  • Helping in formulation of dosage forms

Quick Revision

Physical Pharmacy helps pharmacists understand the physical and chemical properties of drugs and how these properties affect pharmaceutical formulations.
