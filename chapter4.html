Chapter 4: Dissolution

Pharm D • Physical Pharmacy

What is Dissolution?

Dissolution is the process by which a solid substance goes into solution in a suitable solvent or dissolution medium.

Easy Definition: Dissolution means the process of a substance dissolving into a liquid.

Simple Example

When a tablet is placed in water, the drug particles leave the solid tablet and enter the surrounding liquid. This process is called dissolution.

Solubility vs Dissolution

Solubility: How much drug can dissolve under specified conditions.

Dissolution: The process and rate at which the drug goes into solution.

Factors Affecting Dissolution

  • Surface area of the drug particles
  • Temperature
  • Agitation or stirring
  • Solubility of the drug
  • Nature of the dissolution medium
  • Particle size

Pharmaceutical Importance

  • Dissolution affects how quickly a drug becomes available in solution.
  • It is important for tablets and capsules.
  • It can influence drug absorption.
  • Dissolution testing is used in pharmaceutical quality control.
  • It helps compare the performance of dosage forms.
